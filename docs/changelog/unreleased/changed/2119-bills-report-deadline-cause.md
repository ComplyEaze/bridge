- When a request of Tally's Bills Receivable or Payable report takes longer than the 20-second limit, `outstandings` is now refused with its own cause, `native_bills_report_deadline_exceeded`, in place of `request_deadline_exceeded`. Its next step now also says that Tally can go on building the report after the limit, so the next read of that company should wait until `tally_status` answers. Another request of the same read cut at the limit (the groups, the ledgers, the company checks) keeps `request_deadline_exceeded` and no longer gets the Bills report's next step. A call the host withdraws while the read runs now finishes the request in flight and sends nothing more to Tally (part of #2119).
