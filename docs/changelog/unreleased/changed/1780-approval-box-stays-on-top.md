- On Windows, the boxes that ask a person to approve a post, to record a review, to release an invoice stop or to create a ledger are now opened system-modal, so that each stays above Tally when the person clicks into Tally while it is open. When a box opened a few seconds after the person's last input to Tally, Windows had not kept it on top, and one click into a maximised Tally covered it until it was brought back from the taskbar. Here system-modal blocks no other window and does not take the keyboard: after such a click, keys go to Tally (part of #1780).
