What ComplyEaze Bridge can do

The 33 tools release 0.7.0 offers in Claude Desktop, 32 of them with its default settings (posting off) and audit_test_run only when its own setting is ticked, counted by the release’s own check of each published package (it reports 32, and 34 with posting on) and named from the source of that release, and the two that posting adds and the two that master writes adds. Checked against release 0.7.0 on 28 October 2026; the Releases page says which release is the latest.

The 33 tools, as installed

None of them changes your TallyPrime books. Every call adds a receipt line to a log on this computer; the log is designed to hold no figures. Whatever the assistant reads, amounts included, goes to your AI provider.

Two more, only with posting on

Posting is off in a new install. If you turn it on in the extension’s settings, two more tools appear, and each needs you, in a ComplyEaze Bridge window:

  • post_import posts one saved Journal, Payment, Receipt or Contra, or a saved batch of 2 to 50 of them, after you approve it in a ComplyEaze Bridge window (for a batch, its summary), then reads it back.
  • acknowledge_post_review asks you, in its own window, to record that you reviewed a voucher ComplyEaze Bridge posted whose ledger now points to a different master. The assistant cannot approve it for you.

Two more, only with master writes on

A new install does not offer these, and the Claude Desktop extension has no setting that turns them on: they appear only when posting is on and a further setting, set by whoever installs it, is on. They create one new ledger in a company, the first write a published build makes to TallyPrime’s masters, and the second needs you, in a ComplyEaze Bridge window:

  • prepare_ledger_creates reads the company’s ledger and group names, refuses a name that already exists as a ledger, a group or an alias or that differs from one only in case, spaces or symbols, writes nothing to TallyPrime, and returns the ledger as you will be asked to approve it. The ledger is a party under Sundry Debtors or Sundry Creditors, a Suspense ledger, or a GST duty ledger whose head has been measured; it can set no opening balance or parent.
  • post_ledger_creates sends the create once, after you approve it in a ComplyEaze Bridge window, then reads the book back and reports it posted only when exactly one new ledger of that name exists as sent and no other ledger or group changed. It was run once on a synthetic company (TallyPrime 7.1 Silver, 14 October 2026), and its approval window on TallyPrime 7.2 from a test package of an earlier version of the change; the build of this release has not been run, and a create whose answer is lost is not measured.

What it does not do

  • File anything, or match your books with GSTR-1, GSTR-2B or GSTR-3B. The purchase and sales registers are what the books record, not a GST return.
  • Create a ledger unless master writes are on (above), create any other master, or post sales or purchase invoices or GST entries.
  • Undo or delete a voucher it has posted: you correct it in TallyPrime.
  • Reach a TallyPrime on another computer.

The Questions page gives the limits in full, including the reads refused on a larger book, and the README lists what has been run against a real TallyPrime and what has not.
