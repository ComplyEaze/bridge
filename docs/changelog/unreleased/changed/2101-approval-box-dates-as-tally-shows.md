- The approval and review boxes now show dates as Tally's own screens do, for example `14-Sep-2026` and `Books from: 1-Apr-2026`, where they showed Tally's request form `20260914`. This applies to the one-voucher, invoice and batch approval texts and to the one-voucher and batch review texts, on Windows and macOS. Only the text a person reads changes: what is sent to Tally stays `YYYYMMDD`. Each date is up to three characters longer, so a text that sat exactly at a box's size limit before may now be shown as a batch's totals, or refused, as any text over the limit is (fixes #2101).
