- The Bills Receivable and Bills Payable report requests that `outstandings` and the desktop app's outstandings screens send now have a 60-second limit; every other request keeps 20 seconds. On a large book at a year-end date the Bills Receivable report can take longer than 20 seconds, and `outstandings` was then refused for time. Each report is read twice, so such a call can now take well over a minute, and while a Bills request runs, another ComplyEaze Bridge process reading the same Tally (the desktop app among them, behind a call of the MCP server) is refused busy after its usual wait of about 9.5 seconds, and behind the desktop app's own outstandings read another read in the desktop app waits and is refused (`endpoint_queue_deadline_exceeded`) after 30 seconds. That refusal's next step now gives the new limit, and says that its open-bill counts were measured under the earlier 20-second limit. Some hosts give a tool call only about 60 seconds, so the `outstandings` description now says what to do when the host times the call out: tell the user, do not repeat it at once, wait at least a minute, then call `tally_status` once before any other read (part of #2119).
