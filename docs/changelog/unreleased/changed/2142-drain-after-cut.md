- After a request to Tally is cut at its time limit, or otherwise ends without a whole answer, ComplyEaze Bridge now refuses its next request to that Tally, from any of its processes, until Tally has finished: Tally goes on working on a cut request, and a request sent meanwhile only waits behind it and is cut too. The next request first sends `GET /status`, each with its own 5-second limit, and goes ahead once three in a row, 2 seconds apart, have answered in under a second; otherwise it is refused, the request not sent, as `tally_endpoint_draining` with `retry_after_s`, or, after more than two minutes of probes that were not quick, as `tally_endpoint_needs_attention`, which asks the person to look at Tally's screen. Three probes in a row answered whole but slowly clear it too, so a computer that answers every request slowly is not held. A post to the books is refused the same way before its attempt is recorded; its own last step sends no probe. Two lab rows on one synthetic book, with one long read cut at 20 seconds, measured that `/status` waits while Tally works and that a read sent after three quick probes answered at its usual speed; the same through ComplyEaze Bridge's own build has not yet been run live (#2142).
