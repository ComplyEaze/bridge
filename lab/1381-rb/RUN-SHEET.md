No Bridge build: ComplyEaze Bridge's masters runtime refuses Educational Mode, so the half in which Bridge itself refuses the recheck waits for a licensed Tally and is not in this sheet. No backup (R5).

## Computes

`measure.py bind` prints M1's state and whether I1, G1 and U1 are byte-equal to their pins. `measure.py write` prints W's bytes and sha256, whether it is byte-equal to the licensed answer, every counter and LINEERROR by `repr()` as received, and the grade (R6), or `WRITE NOT PARSED` when Tally answered but the answer does not parse. `measure.py state M2` takes its expected mark from that grade. `measure.py aim` prints I2's rows, whether each is bound to the book, and whether I2 is byte-equal to the licensed read after the same write. `measure.py all` prints, from the files alone, per label: bytes, sha256 and its rows, or `NOT READ` with its reason (never zero rows); the three states, the bind and the aim (a row that cannot be read there is `NOT READ: <what>: <reason>`, never met or missed); M3, I3, G3 and U3 against the pins, byte-equal and rows equal (rows compared as canonical XML: tag, attributes, trimmed text, children in order); and `RESTORE UNDID THE WRITE: yes` only when all four are byte-equal. `restore.py after` prints the data-folder listing difference: entries added, removed and changed (bytes or modified time), by name.
