# 2018 Stock Query, three open questions (#2018, #2017): item names with special characters, an alternate unit, and the stock items collection's closing balance at other period ends

About 1 hour 45 minutes. Part A READS ONLY. Part W WRITES: seven stock items are created by hand on Tally's screens (a recorded write), read, and the book is restored from a backup; ComplyEaze Bridge only reads. Not for merge. `SHA256.txt` lists every file here with its bytes; check it before step 0.

## What it decides
A planned read-only `stock_by_batch` tool (#2017, #2018) rests on `lab/2018-stock-query-with-item-answers` and `lab/2018-stock-query-items-periods-answers`: report ID `Stock Query` with the static variable `STOCKITEMNAME` returns closing quantity per godown and batch for one item, follows the period end, and sums to the item's closing. Three things are not measured and the tool would refuse them until they are:
1. **Item names with characters beyond letters, digits and spaces.** The 11 items of this book have plain names, and no committed lab capture holds a stock item with `&`, a quotation mark, an apostrophe, a parenthesis, `%`, `/` or `,` in its name (a scan of 1,617 files on the lab answers branches on 28 Oct found one name with a full stop, in a demo import file, and none of the others). Part W therefore creates seven items and sends their names, escaped once as XML text, to Stock Query.
2. **An alternate unit.** The quantity text of every answer so far is one number and one unit. Part W creates an item with an alternate unit and reads how the answer writes it.
3. **The stock items collection's closing balance at other period ends.** The tool would tie each item's lines to the collection's `CLOSINGBALANCE`; the committed fixture holds that only for the year. Part A sends the collection at three period ends and Stock Query for five items at two of them, and compares.
It decides no tool, field or estimate.

## What the row records, and what it does not
For each created name: whether Tally stored it as typed, how the collection and Stock Query write it, and whether each escape form selects the item; for the alternate unit: the quantity text; for the other period ends: the collection's closing balance against the Stock Query lines. **Confidence:** one observation per name, PARTIAL until repeated. Not covered: detecting an alternate-unit item by a field (a new FETCH would be a new request shape), other punctuation, names of other lengths or with leading or trailing spaces, a batch-kept item with such a name, an alternate-unit voucher, a book that is not batch-wise, the tax-audit book. It decides no alphabet for a tool: it records what these names do.
