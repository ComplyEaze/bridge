- The Bills Receivable and Bills Payable report requests that `outstandings` sends now have a 60-second limit; every other request keeps 20 seconds. On a large book at a year-end date the Bills Receivable report can take longer than 20 seconds, and `outstandings` was then refused for time. Each report is read twice, so such a call can now take well over a minute, and while a Bills request runs, another ComplyEaze Bridge process reading the same Tally is refused busy after its usual wait, and in the desktop app another read of the same Tally waits behind it and is refused (`endpoint_queue_deadline_exceeded`) after 30 seconds. That refusal's next step now gives the new limit, and says that its open-bill counts were measured under the earlier 20-second limit (part of #2119).
