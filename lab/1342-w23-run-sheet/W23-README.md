*For the reader (not for the lab):* the uncertain-voucher list, `gst_status` and the invoice readback all read a Sales type by its exact name and were measured on invoices. A credit note is the other half of a GSTR-1 period: this set records what the same read returns for one (its three status flags, whether it is cancelled, optional or post-dated, and the sign of its amount), so that a list that names a credit-note type does not report it by the invoice's rules.

Pre-registered expectations (written before any answer):
- (a) `aw23-a.xml` and `aw23-c.xml` hold no voucher and are byte-identical; `aw23-marks-2.xml` equals `aw23-marks-0.xml`; `aw23-series-2.xml` equals `aw23-series-0.xml`.
- (b) `aw23-b.xml` holds exactly one voucher, of type `Credit Note`, with party `BRIDGE Walk-in` and an `AMOUNT` of magnitude 210.00. The sign of the amount (an invoice reads negative), the three status flags, `ISGSTOVERRIDDEN`, `ISCANCELLED`, `ISOPTIONAL`, `ISPOSTDATED`, `ISDELETED` and `ISDELETEDVCHRETAINED` are recorded, not predicted; so is whether the answer carries the ledger entries as an invoice's does.
- (c) the marks of `aw23-marks-1.xml` differ from the first by the one voucher (ALTVCHID plus 1); whether the `Credit Note` row of `aw23-series-1.xml` differs from the first is recorded, not predicted.

The opposite of any of these is a finding, not an error. Not covered: a credit note against a registered buyer, with items, with a different tax rate or reason, in a foreign currency, or posted by ComplyEaze Bridge; a debit note; a cancelled credit note.
