- When a Sales invoice is refused because a voucher already in the book has its date, ledgers, amounts and sides, and the person says it is another sale, the next step no longer tells them to enter the invoice in Tally by hand. Where the voucher has no number, it says ComplyEaze Bridge cannot post that sale yet: leave it out and tell the user. Where an earlier invoice batch from this computer is still unconfirmed, it says the refusal ends only when `verify_import` confirms that batch, and until then to leave the invoice out and tell the user. Neither says to enter it by hand; the refusal, its fields and the case of an invoice number already in use are unchanged (#1709).
