- `vouchers` takes `include_gst_details`. With it, each ledger entry carries `gst_details` as Tally sent it on the entry: its taxability and type of supply, each absent, empty or a value, and its rates, one per duty head. Each rate is absent, empty, zero or a value with its text as sent, read as `ledger_masters` reads a ledger's own rates, and the four are never merged. These are the entry's own details, not the tax charged; the tax entries' amounts are that. An entry whose details cannot be read whole carries `gst_details_unreadable` with a reason, and the rest of the answer is unchanged. Without the argument the answer is as before (part of #2027).
