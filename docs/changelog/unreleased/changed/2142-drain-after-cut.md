- After a request to Tally is cut at its time limit, or otherwise ends without a whole answer, ComplyEaze Bridge now waits for Tally to finish before its next request to that Tally, from any of its processes: Tally goes on working on a cut request, and a request sent meanwhile only waits behind it and is cut too. The next request first sends `GET /status`, each with its own 5-second limit, and goes ahead once three in a row, 2 seconds apart, have answered in under a second; otherwise it is refused, having sent nothing, as `tally_endpoint_draining` with `retry_after_s`, or, long after the cut, as `tally_endpoint_needs_attention`, which asks the person to look at Tally's screen. A post to the books meets such a wait as a refusal before its attempt is recorded, with no probe. Two lab rows on one synthetic book, with one long read cut at 20 seconds, measured that `/status` waits while Tally works and that a read sent after three quick probes answered at its usual speed; the same through ComplyEaze Bridge's own build has not yet been run live (#2142).
