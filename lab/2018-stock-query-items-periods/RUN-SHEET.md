## What it decides
`lab/2018-stock-query-with-item-answers` found that the report ID `Stock Query` with the static variable `STOCKITEMNAME` set to an item answers with the item's last purchase and one godown / batch / quantity line per batch (`Hydrochloric Acid 33pc`: Main Location / Primary Batch 150.000 Kgs, Main Location / H-2504-A 300.000 Kgs, Factory Floor / H-2504-B 366.667 Kgs, summing to the item's closing 816.667 Kgs), and that a bare request, another variable name and an item the book lacks all answer the same empty envelope (46 bytes). It tried one item and one period (1 April 2025 to 31 March 2026). Not shown: (a) whether the lines follow the period (`SVFROMDATE`, `SVTODATE`) or are always the book's state; (b) what the answer holds for the other items of the book: items kept in batches with stock (Caustic Soda Flakes 400 Kgs, Cleaning Kit A 12 Nos, Sulphuric Acid 98pc 100 Kgs), an item kept in batches with no stock (Descaler Concentrate), items kept without batches (Soda Ash Light with 1,300 Kgs, of which (-)50.000 Kgs sit in Factory Floor; Carton Box Small; HDPE Drum 50L), items with no stock and no batches (Cleaning Kit B, Label Roll; "no stock" rests on an empty closing balance in the 30 Sep stock-item fixture and `none_sent` in the 2017/2018 tool answer) and an item with negative stock (Zero Stock Item, (-)50.000 Kgs on the Stock Summary). This sheet sends the eleven items at the year, and three items at the opening day (Hydrochloric Acid 33pc, Zero Stock Item, Cleaning Kit A: items whose opening differs from their closing), and reads the answers against Tally's Stock Query screen for the items step 5 names. It decides no read, tool name or estimate for #2018 or #2017.
