- The tax audit's loans and interest test now counts the TDS on a voucher whose only lines are two or more loans on the same side and TDS netting to their sum: each loan takes its own line as its TDS, and the loan's own check no longer reports such a voucher as dropped. A voucher whose TDS lines net to a debit is no longer netted into the TDS deducted on the loan, because the debit can reverse an earlier deduction. The figure names each such voucher, and its debits are shown as their own figure. Both change how the s.194A coverage reads. A loan whose TDS includes such a split voucher now reads a new value, "not checked", never "covered": the loan is listed for clause 21(b) with that limit, because the books do not say how the TDS divides between the loans' interest. A loan that had a TDS debit beside its deductions can now read differently, because the TDS deducted no longer has the debit netted in. These are declared differences from the reference engine (#1875).
