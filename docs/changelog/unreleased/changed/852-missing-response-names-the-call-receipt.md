- `verify_import` and `post_import` now add a next step to the reconciliation-required error of a batch with no record of Tally's answer to its post. It says that `egress_log` returns the most recent receipts; that the sending `post_import` call's receipt, when it is recent enough to be among them, lists the last 32 sends with their `held_ms` and outcome but does not mark which send was the post; that a later `post_import` or `verify_import` call on the batch does not post it again and leaves its own receipt, holding only that call's reads; and that a receipt not returned may be older than `egress_log` reads, or may never have been written, as when the process ended first (part of #852).
