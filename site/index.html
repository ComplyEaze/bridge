A thick case file is followed through eight short chapters, as a 3D book where the browser can draw one. It arrives with twenty-six sticky index flags down its edge, coloured by the kind of finding (flagging findings is in development and not in the download). Asked who owes money for more than 90 days, the flags fold back into the edge, it opens to an ageing page, and the five overdue parties are ringed in pencil. The page turns to a voucher, two pencil ticks are drawn, and its 40A(3) flag leaves the page and lands as row 1 of a working paper titled “Working paper · Tax audit FY 2025-26” (flagging is in development and not in the download). The page turns again to a bank statement; a slip lifts off each of five rows and the pile reads 139 matched, and three question-mark flags mark near-miss ledger names that wait for your decision; you decide them one after another, each turns into a tick, and the count of lines matched to your ledgers rises from 139 to 142; ComplyEaze Bridge then prepares one receipt as its own one-voucher file, and the other 141 vouchers as a file for you to import in Tally. A replica of the approval window of release 0.3.0 on a Mac rises from that receipt's row, asks you to post that one voucher, and shrinks back into the row with a red tick: one voucher per approval, and a voucher is posted this way or imported in a file, never both. After you import the file of the other 141 in Tally yourself, each voucher is ticked as ComplyEaze Bridge reads it back, counting up to 142 of 142: 141 imported and one posted. A narrow receipt slip slides out for the calls made. Finally the flags retract, the file shuts on one tick, and the five install steps and the download follow.

Set it up in five steps.

  1. In TallyPrime, turn on the HTTP gateway (Tally acts as “Both”).
  2. Open the downloaded .mcpb file. If Claude Desktop doesn't pick it up: Settings → Extensions → Advanced settings → Install Extension.
  3. Check the Tally port in the extension settings (usually 9000).
  4. Read the Terms of Use linked in the extension settings, then turn on “I accept the ComplyEaze Bridge Terms of Use”.
  5. Save the settings, then quit Claude Desktop completely and reopen it. Then ask about your books in plain language.
