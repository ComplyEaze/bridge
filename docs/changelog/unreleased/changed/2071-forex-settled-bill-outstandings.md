- `outstandings` no longer refuses with `native_amount_invalid` on a book whose parties are kept in rupees but hold bills entered in a foreign currency. Tally sends a bill settled in full at another rate with an empty closing amount, and such a party is already set aside, so its rows are now dropped before their amounts are read. Where a rupee party's bill is open, the answer reports the rupee bills and names the parties set aside. Where every open bill belongs to a set-aside party, the answer is partial and gives no figure. An empty closing amount for a party that is not set aside still refuses. A balance whose foreign-currency rate has decimals still refuses the read, as before (#2071).
