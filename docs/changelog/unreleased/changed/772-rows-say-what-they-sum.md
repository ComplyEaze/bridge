- A s.40A(3) row on a payee's ledger now says it sums that ledger's debits on vouchers with a cash
  payment, and that the cash paid is not shown, instead of calling the amount cash paid. In the
  high-value register a bank row says the money was received into or paid from the bank. A bank row
  with no party ledger no longer asks whether one person reached the vouching threshold, and its
  label names the bank leg. A single-reference row with no party ledger says it sums the payers' or
  payees' side. No row or figure value changes (#772).
