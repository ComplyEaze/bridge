# Windows item 1633-L4 v1: whether deleting a voucher takes an AlterID or frees its MasterID, and whether a GSTR-1 export's rise of the voucher mark is skipped by the next voucher. (26 Oct 2026.) SYNTHETIC ONLY: THE COMPANY is `BRIDGE SPAN MARK LAB`, created by the owner in step 1 of this item. The owner keys and deletes vouchers on Tally's own screens; ComplyEaze Bridge only reads: nothing is posted or imported. LAB-COMMON v3 applies in full, except where "Differences" says otherwise; where the two differ, the stricter holds.

Why: issue 1633. A native post binds the vouchers it created to the AlterIDs between the company's voucher mark (`ALTVCHID`) before the post and after it. A GSTR-1 export was measured once (TallyPrime 7.1) to raise that mark with no voucher change, which makes a correct post read as unverified. A proposal to bind the post's vouchers anyway when the mark rises by more than the post created was set aside until three premises are measured: whether a deleted voucher's AlterID is taken from the same sequence, whether Tally gives a new voucher the MasterID of a deleted one, and whether the next voucher after an export skips the AlterIDs the export took. Nothing in this item is built on: it records what Tally does (AGENTS.md P1, P6).
