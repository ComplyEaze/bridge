# W1 (#917): what is the longest ledger, group and company name TallyPrime accepts, in characters and in bytes?
WHY: the ledger-count census sizes its slices on an assumed worst-case name of 128 characters (the longest seen in the read-only survey was 88). The assumption is UNVERIFIED; if it is wrong by more than a factor of two a response can pass the transport's 32 MiB cap and Bridge abandons the read. This measures the limit. Only a 150-character company name is held today.
RULES: LAB-COMMON v3 C1 to C11 apply in full (THE COMPANY loaded alone; step 0; backup before the first keying; restore only on the coordinator's word; screens and dialogs; STOP and close). Every read below is sent by the built tool, one at a time, so no hand-written request is sent and the request-ID preflight does not apply. Licensed mode only; Educational mode anywhere = STOP.
COMPANY: BRIDGE NAMELEN LAB (new, THE COMPANY), loaded alone, no voucher. If a company or data folder of this name exists on the PC, STOP and report it. Create it with books beginning 1 April 2025 (OWNER STEP); take backup BK1 into a NEW empty folder (name, size, sha256) before the first keying (C5). Nothing is ever sent to a client book.
MODE: B. Posting OFF (reads only; the owner keys).
WHAT IS KEPT: every answer whole with its size and sha256; for each name tried: its length in characters, its length in UTF-8 bytes, and the screen's result (accepted as typed, cut to N characters, or refused).
STEPS (OWNER STEP = the owner works Tally's screen, one step at a time; no lane touches it):
1. `list_companies`: expect exactly BRIDGE NAMELEN LAB, loaded alone; STOP before any call otherwise. GUID. `tally_status`: M0, V0.
2. LEDGER NAME, ASCII. OWNER STEP: create a ledger named with 1 letter "A" repeated 64 times under Sundry Debtors. Then, one variable at a time, create ledgers with 99, 100, 128, 129, 160 and 255 repeated letters (letters differ by position in the last two characters so the names are distinct; each name's exact text is written in the notes before it is keyed). After each, record whether Tally accepted it as typed, cut it, or refused. Stop at the first refusal or cut. `ledger_masters` after the series (limit 50): record each name as returned, its length in characters, and whether a cut name is the first N characters of the typed one.
3. LEDGER NAME, characters that are more than one byte (one variable changed: the character set). OWNER STEP: repeat the series at the longest accepted ASCII length L, with the letter "é" (U+00E9, two bytes in UTF-8) repeated L times, then L/2 + 1 times, and record whether the limit counts characters or bytes.
4. GROUP NAME. OWNER STEP: the same series (64, then the lengths of step 2 up to its first refusal) for a group under Primary. `masters` with `kind=groups`: record names as returned.
5. COMPANY NAME. OWNER STEP: do NOT rename THE COMPANY. Create ONE further new company, BRIDGE NAMELEN COMPANY TEST, with books from 1 April 2025, named with the same series where the screen allows one (record the longest accepted); shut it; load THE COMPANY alone again (C1). `list_companies` must show only THE COMPANY. A company name is never changed in place.
6. The longest accepted ledger name from step 2 made of apostrophes (each is six characters once escaped in the answer): OWNER STEP create it; `ledger_masters` limit 1 for it: record the egress line's response bytes and the name as returned. This is an informational size only; the census row formula needs the census request, which is not sent here.
7. `tally_status`: M1, V1. C10 close.
EXPECT (record, not predicted): the accepted maximum for each of the three kinds, whether it counts characters or bytes, what Tally does at the first over-long name (cut or refused), and the name as the read returns it.
PASS = the three limits are recorded with the evidence of each boundary (the longest accepted and the first refused or cut). FAIL (a finding) = a name returned differently from what the screen accepted, or a read that fails on a long name (report whole). A name cut silently is a finding: record both strings with their lengths.
AFTER: OWNER STEP: restore BK1 on the run coordinator's explicit RESTORE; marks equal M0, V0 and the keyed names are gone from `ledger_masters`.
STOP RULES: C7 (any dialog, no Escape on master screens); a read that does not answer or answers outside the stated shape; any second company loaded while a name is being keyed in THE COMPANY. Never test a name on a client book.
