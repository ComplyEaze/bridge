## 9. AI assistants, posting and approvals

9.1 **AI output can be wrong.** Your AI Assistant decides which Bridge tools to use and how to read the results. It can misread figures, pick the wrong company, period or ledger, leave things out, or state things that are not true. Bridge reports some limits and refuses some requests. It does not check your AI Assistant's reasoning or conclusions.

9.2 **Posting.** Posting is off by default in new installations. If you upgraded from an earlier version, check the setting. If you turn posting on, you accept the known limits described in the extension settings and in the documentation published with your Build. These include:

- **Wrong company or ledger.** If a company or ledger is renamed, replaced or loaded in Tally at the moment of posting, the voucher could land in a different company or ledger than the one Bridge checked. Do not change companies or ledgers in Tally while a posting is waiting.
- **Other changes.** Bridge cannot stop changes made directly in Tally, or by other software, while it works.
- **Tally slowdowns.** Bridge's requests can make Tally slow or unresponsive, sometimes until Tally is restarted. This can affect other users of a shared Tally.
- **No undo.** **Bridge has no tool to delete, reverse or undo a voucher it has posted.** You must correct a wrong posting yourself in Tally.

9.3 **Your approval is your instruction.** Each posting waits for your approval in a separate window on your computer. The window shows what will be posted: for a single voucher, its entries; for a batch (where a Build allows batches), a summary. An approval is used once and stays valid for up to 15 minutes after you give it.

When you approve, you instruct Bridge to post what the window showed. For a batch, you instruct Bridge to post every voucher in the batch that the summary describes. You are responsible for that decision and its accounting consequences, whoever proposed the voucher: you, your AI Assistant or anyone else. This does not apply to any difference between what the window showed and what Bridge actually posted. Do not approve a voucher you have not read and understood.

9.4 **Safeguards.** Bridge gives your AI Assistant no way to approve a posting. Approving postings automatically, or otherwise getting around the approval window or other safeguards, removes their protection. If you, or software acting for you (including software that can control your screen), do so, we are not responsible for the result.

9.5 **Checking after posting is not an audit.** After posting, Bridge reads the voucher back from Tally and reports what it found. A match means the details Bridge compared were the same. Bridge does not compare every detail. A match does not mean the entry is correct, complete, properly classified or compliant.
