# 2068 control, an integrated book (#2068, Part of #692): what `trial_balance` shows of the stock on a book whose stock is integrated with its accounts, beside Tally's own Trial Balance, read only

Not for merge. About 20 minutes. Read only: no key is pressed in Tally except to open and view, nothing is created, altered, imported or restored, and no backup is needed (C5 and C6 do not apply: the book does not change). `SHA256.txt` lists every file here with its bytes; check it before step 0.

## What it decides
`lab/2068-nonint-trial-balance-answers` measured, on a book with "Integrate Accounts with Inventory" set to No, that `trial_balance` reports the Closing Stock ledger (under Stock-in-Hand) at its opening value, so that its opening + debit + credit does not equal its closing (400.00 apart). #2068 asks whether an answer should say so. The synthetic book this sheet reads, `BRIDGE TIEOUT D`, is integrated and holds its stock only as a stock item: the ledger collection recorded for it has seven ledgers and none is under Stock-in-Hand. So this sheet cannot repeat the non-integrated case. It asks the question the book can answer: on an integrated book with stock, does Tally's own Trial Balance carry a stock line that `trial_balance` does not, and does the tool's totals gap (opening and closing sums) equal that line? A true control, a ledger keyed under Stock-in-Hand on an integrated book, is a write and a sheet of its own; it is not this one.
