- The tax audit's loans and interest test now counts the TDS on a voucher that debits two or more loans together against TDS alone, where that TDS nets to their sum: each loan takes its own line as its TDS, and the loan's own check no longer reports such a voucher as dropped. A voucher whose TDS lines net to a debit is no longer netted into the TDS deducted on the loan, because the debit can reverse an earlier deduction. The figure names each such voucher, and its debits are shown as their own figure. These are declared differences from the reference engine (#1875).
