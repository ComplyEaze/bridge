# G1 (#1039): do a post's MasterIDs and GUIDs survive an edit on Tally's screen, and what happens when another user writes during a post?
WHY: a native post is attributed by binding its vouchers to their own AlterID span, and a refused binding is final for the batch. The design read for #1039 (a person-made binding, or a machine rebind for a one-voucher post) rests on facts nobody has measured: (a) after a voucher is edited on Tally's screen, do its MasterID and GUID stay what the post's own answer implied (GUID = company GUID + MasterID, MasterID inside the span the answer's LASTVCHID ends); (b) with a second user writing, does LASTVCHID name Bridge's own voucher; (c) does a second writer produce `span_step_not_created` or `span_position_mismatch`. A refusal has never been reproduced in a lab run. This row measures; it builds nothing.
RULES: LAB-COMMON v3 C1 to C11 apply in full. Every call is made by the built tool, one at a time; no hand-written request is sent, so the request-ID preflight does not apply. Posting is ON; the owner answers each approval window after the call returns. Licensed mode only (posts and keying on an ordinary day); Educational mode anywhere = STOP. Rows B and C need a multi-user edition (Gold) with a second Tally client on the same company: STOP before them if the edition is not multi-user, and report it (row A alone still runs).
COMPANY: BRIDGE CONCUR LAB (new, THE COMPANY), loaded alone: a bank ledger BRIDGE Lab Bank, two party ledgers BRIDGE Conc Party 1 and 2 (Sundry Debtors), voucher type Receipt Automatic with "Retain Original Voucher No.". If a company or data folder of this name exists on the PC, STOP and report it. Create it with books beginning 1 April 2025 (OWNER STEP); take backup BK1 into a NEW empty folder (name, size, sha256) before the first keying (C5). Small by construction: at most 40 vouchers.
ROW A (single user): an edit on Tally's screen.
A1. `list_companies`: expect exactly BRIDGE CONCUR LAB, loaded alone; STOP before any call otherwise. GUID. `tally_status`: M0, V0.
A2. `build_import_xml`: ONE Receipt of 1.00 from BRIDGE Conc Party 1 into BRIDGE Lab Bank on 10 April 2025, narration "G1-A". `post_import` (owner approves). `verify_import`: expect `posted_verified` and `post_span_binding.state` `bound`. Record the whole answers. `vouchers` for 10 April 2025: record MasterID, AlterID, GUID, number. `tally_status`: M1, V1.
A3. OWNER STEP: on Tally's screen alter that voucher's narration to "G1-A edited" and save (never Escape on a voucher screen; record the prompts). `tally_status`: M2, V2. `vouchers` for 10 April 2025: record MasterID, AlterID, GUID. 
A4. `verify_import` of the same batch again: record the whole answer (expect a divergence, not `posted_verified`; record the status and next step).
EXPECT (record, not predicted): MasterID and GUID unchanged by the edit; AlterID moved (by how much); the voucher mark moved (by how much). The GUID equals the company GUID followed by the MasterID as 8 hex digits (record the pair).
PASS = both tables recorded whole, the three deltas and the GUID relation stated. A MasterID or GUID that changed on an edit is a finding that ends the person-binding and machine-rebind designs: report at once.
ROW B (Gold, a second user): one-voucher posts with another writer.
B1. OWNER STEP: the second user connects to BRIDGE CONCUR LAB from a second Tally client; record the edition screen and both clients' titles. `list_companies`: still exactly one company.
B2. Ten attempts, each: `build_import_xml` ONE Receipt of 1.00 (Party 1 or 2 alternately, narration "G1-B-n", dated 10 April 2025), `post_import`; the owner answers the approval window; at the same moment OWNER STEP: the second user saves a Receipt of 1.00 on the screen (a prepared voucher, accepted at the moment the approval is answered; record the clock time of both acts to the second). Then `verify_import`: record `post_span_binding` (state, code, current mark) and the status. Then `vouchers` for 10 April 2025: record MasterID, AlterID, GUID of the batch's voucher and of the neighbour keyed by the second user. Record the answer's LASTVCHID if the post's result shows it (the egress line or the result's dispatch outcome).
B3. After the ten attempts, tabulate: how many bound, how many refused and with which code, and for each whether LASTVCHID was Bridge's voucher or the second user's.
ROW C (Gold): a three-voucher batch with another writer.
C1. Three attempts as B2 but with a saved batch of 3 Receipts (narrations "G1-C-n-1..3"); the second user saves one Receipt during the post. Record the same, plus whether the batch reads `reconciliation_required` and with which doubt (`batch_step_unconfirmed` or a refusal code).
PASS (rows B and C) = every attempt recorded with the two clock times and the answers; the bound and refused counts stated; whether LASTVCHID ever named the second user's voucher. Not reproducing a refusal is a result: record "not reproduced in N attempts". An attempt where the post is refused or the dialog times out is recorded whole, not repeated.
AFTER: OWNER STEP: the second user disconnects; restore BK1 on the run coordinator's explicit RESTORE. C10 close.
STOP RULES: C7; step A2 not `posted_verified`/`bound` (report whole, do not post again); a voucher count other than expected after any attempt (report whole before the next attempt); a mark that moves outside a step that changes it.
