Using Claude on a client's Tally books: where the data goes

Written on 2 October 2026, about release 0.4.1. If a newer release is out, its release notes, the README, the security page, the Terms of Use and the Privacy Policy are the current text; this post is not edited when they change.

When you ask Claude about a client's books, what Claude reads goes to Anthropic. That can be party names, narrations, GSTINs and amounts. A setting can shorten the names. Nothing hides the amounts.

We do not receive your Tally data through ComplyEaze Bridge, and our Privacy Policy says so. We looked for an upload in the code and found none. We read release 0.4.0 and checked the 0.4.1 changes to network destinations. That was reading the source. We have not measured the running program, and nobody outside has audited it.

ComplyEaze Bridge is an open-source extension for Claude Desktop. It reads the TallyPrime open on your own computer: outstanding receivables and payables with ageing, the trial balance, profit and loss, balance sheet, ledger movement, vouchers in a date window, a purchase register and closing stock. Some of these reads refuse a book rather than answer in part, and books with several currencies or many stock items have further limits. It can also check ledger names against your book and prepare Journal, Payment, Receipt and Contra vouchers as a file. We are still developing it, and it is not yet code-signed, so your computer may warn you before opening it. We publish it for Windows (x64) and Apple Silicon Macs, with TallyPrime on the same computer. On a Mac, TallyPrime runs in a Windows virtual machine, with the connection forwarded locally.

Follow one question

Say you type "What does Demo Traders owe me?" in Claude Desktop.

  1. You ask Claude Desktop.
  2. Claude calls a tool in ComplyEaze Bridge, which runs on your computer.
  3. The tool asks TallyPrime through Tally's own connection, at an address that means "this computer". It cannot be pointed at a Tally on another machine, and it cannot tell whether you have forwarded that address elsewhere, so do not forward it across the internet.
  4. Tally answers, and a line goes into a log on your computer.
  5. The answer goes to Claude Desktop. It holds the party's name and the amount.
  6. Claude Desktop sends it to your AI provider, as part of the conversation. This is where your data leaves the computer. We know of one other route, the flaw described further down. We have captured no network traffic, so there may be others.

Depending on what Claude asks for, what reaches your provider can include company, ledger and party names, vouchers with dates, amounts, narrations and GSTINs, outstanding bills, the trial balance and other statements, PAN and bank account numbers, the contents of a bank-statement PDF you ask it to read, and file locations that usually include your computer user name.

Whether your provider keeps this, trains on it or processes it elsewhere depends on the provider, your plan and your settings. We do not know yours. Ask three things: Is it kept? Is it used for training? Where is it processed?

ComplyEaze Bridge has one masking setting, "Response redaction". It can shorten party and ledger names to their first two and last two characters, or it can remove narrations, but not both. Neither makes the data anonymous. Amounts, company names, GSTINs and PAN numbers are never masked. Out of the box, nothing is masked.

Our Terms and Privacy Policy put one more thing on you. You need each client's authority to access their books with ComplyEaze Bridge and to share their data with your AI provider, and you must give any notice or get any consent that the law or your engagement requires. Your duties of confidentiality still apply.

What stays on your computer

The log from step 4 records the time, the tool, Tally's internal code for the company, and short codes ("fingerprints") of the request and the result. It is designed to hold no amounts or names, though sizes can hint at a name's length, so treat it as confidential. The import journal, the import files and the bank-statement proposals do hold amounts and names, unmasked.

None of it is deleted automatically, and removing the extension may leave it behind. Do not simply delete the folder. ComplyEaze Bridge uses it to avoid sending the same batch twice, so the Privacy Policy tells you to check Tally and then archive it in an encrypted archive. Administrators and backup software on the computer may be able to read these files, and on Windows they take the folder's normal permissions. Use a device password and full-disk encryption.

What it can do to your books

Reading does not write to your books. A large read can still slow Tally, sometimes until Tally is restarted, and that can affect other people on a shared Tally.

Posting is off in a new installation. If you upgraded, check the setting, because an earlier default may still be saved as on. When it is on, each voucher (Journal, Payment, Receipt or Contra) waits for your approval in a separate window on your computer. An approval is used once and expires. No ComplyEaze Bridge tool lets the assistant approve a post, but software that can control your screen could still click the button, so do not let such software run while a posting is waiting.

A post can land in the wrong place if a company or ledger is renamed, replaced or loaded in Tally at that moment, so leave companies and ledgers alone while a post is waiting. The tool cannot undo a posted voucher; you correct it in Tally. After posting it reads the voucher back, and a match does not mean the entry is correct or compliant. It does not create masters or post sales, purchase, tax or inventory entries.

A flaw in 0.3.0 and 0.4.0, fixed in 0.4.1

In releases 0.3.0 and 0.4.0 the bank-statement tool could be given a Windows network path, meaning a file on another computer. Windows then connects to that computer and may send the signed-in user's network sign-in response to it. No Tally data is sent. It needed the assistant to be talked into using such a path, your approval of that call or an "always allow" setting, and outbound file-sharing traffic to that computer. Release 0.4.1 refuses these paths, but a drive letter mapped to a network share, a link, and on a Mac a mounted network volume can still pass. Keep statements and password files on the computer's own disk. If you have 0.3.0 or 0.4.0, update. ComplyEaze Bridge does not check for updates itself, so look at the releases page now and then. The advisory is linked from our security policy.

What we have run

On 1 October we installed a candidate build of 0.4.0 on a Windows 11 computer, with a new Claude account that had no paid plan, TallyPrime Silver 7.1 and one made-up company. With the Terms setting off we called a tool; it refused and read nothing. We switched the setting on, and six tools answered. We declined one post in the approval window, and nothing was sent. We approved another: Tally took one Journal, and the read-back found it. We kept notes and screenshots and have not published them. It was one run, and we have not run each way of declining in the Windows approval window (we tried one) in a controlled test.

We have not yet run the published 0.4.1 file in Claude Desktop, and not every read has its own recorded run. The README lists what we ran, on which build, and what we have not, for example posting with the published package against a live TallyPrime. Reads on very large books can fail or take longer than the assistant waits.

Take a backup, try a test company first, and check what you get in Tally before you rely on it. Read the Terms of Use, including section 14 on liability. We do not promise support or updates. Nothing here is accounting, tax, audit or legal advice.

हिंदी में सार (अंग्रेज़ी पाठ ही लागू होता है)

  • ComplyEaze Bridge के ज़रिए हमें आपका Tally डेटा नहीं मिलता; हमारी Privacy Policy यही कहती है। हमने जो कोड पढ़ा (0.4.0, और 0.4.1 के network बदलाव), उसमें कोई upload नहीं मिला। चलते program को हमने नापा नहीं है।
  • Claude जो पढ़ता है, वह आपके AI provider (Claude के लिए Anthropic) को जाता है: कंपनी और पार्टी के नाम, narration, GSTIN, रकम, और कभी-कभी PAN व बैंक विवरण भी। "Response redaction" सेटिंग पार्टी और ledger के नाम छोटे कर सकती है या narration हटा सकती है, दोनों साथ नहीं; रकम पर यह कभी लागू नहीं होती। Provider डेटा रखता है या नहीं, उस पर training करता है या नहीं, कहाँ process करता है, यह उसकी शर्तों में देखें।
  • क्लाइंट की बुक्स पर इस्तेमाल करने से पहले क्लाइंट की authority आपके पास होनी चाहिए। Confidentiality की आपकी ज़िम्मेदारी भी बनी रहती है।
  • आपके कंप्यूटर पर रखे import के journal, import फ़ाइलों और bank statement के proposals में रकम और नाम पूरे दिखते हैं, और ये अपने-आप नहीं मिटते।
  • नई installation में posting बंद रहती है। पुराने version से upgrade किया हो तो setting जाँचें। चालू करने पर हर voucher आपकी मंज़ूरी का इंतज़ार करता है। उसी समय किसी कंपनी या ledger का नाम बदले, उसकी जगह दूसरा आ जाए, या Tally में दूसरी कंपनी load हो, तो voucher गलत जगह जा सकता है। जो software आपकी screen पर click कर सकता है, वह मंज़ूरी का बटन भी दबा सकता है; posting के इंतज़ार में ऐसा software न चलाएँ। Post हुआ voucher यह टूल वापस नहीं ले सकता; Tally में खुद सुधारें।
  • 0.3.0 और 0.4.0 के bank statement टूल में network path की खामी थी। 0.4.1 ऐसे path रोकता है, पर mapped drive, link और Mac पर mounted network volume अब भी निकल सकते हैं। पुराना version हो तो update करें, और statement अपने कंप्यूटर की डिस्क पर रखें।
  • पहले backup लें और test company पर आज़माएँ। Tally में नतीजा खुद जाँचें। यह अभी code-signed नहीं है। यह accounting, tax, audit या कानूनी सलाह नहीं है।
