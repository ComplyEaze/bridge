- `gst_by_counter_ledger` no longer totals a supplier invoice twice when it was keyed twice in Tally. A Purchase entered against the supplier bill that another Purchase in the window opened, for the same supplier ledger, is set aside as `repeats_a_purchase_bill`, with its own tax totals, and its listing names the first copy in `repeats`; the first copy stays attributed. A second copy whose first is outside the window, or that was keyed with a bill of its own or with none, is still not seen (#2069).
