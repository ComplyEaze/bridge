Every call here passes `as_of` 20260831 so that `party_gstin_as_of` is fixed, and step 6 compares two `as_of` values only to see that the mailing list does not move. The workbook (step 9) is what picks the entry in force on a date.
3. **Marks.** `tally_status` carries no marks. M (ALTMSTID) and V (ALTVCHID) are read with `ledger_masters` (first page, no `snapshot_id`): `snapshot.master_alter_id` and `snapshot.voucher_alter_id`; record the whole answer and `snapshot.reused`, which must read false. Reads are compared by their items, totals and marks, never as whole answers (the snapshot id and read time change with each read).
4. **Backup and restore.** One backup, BK1, of THE COMPANY alone before the first keying (C5), into a new empty folder `C:\bridge-lab\1490\backups\bk1\`. C6's fallback backup is BK1 itself: no second backup is taken, and a restore that fails its checks stops the sheet with the book as it stands (step 8).
5. **Grant.** Steps 5 and 7 key on Tally's screen: a second dated mailing entry on `Lab Party Duplicate Probe`, and the attempt of step 7. Nothing else is written, by Tally's screen or by import. Posting and ledger creation by ComplyEaze Bridge are not enabled.
