- The tax audit's trial balance test now says, for a ledger whose closing balance is not its opening plus its debits less its credits, how far apart they are: a new figure `tb_closing_not_movements_<tag>` (the closing less opening plus debit less credit, debit positive), beside the closing balance, which is still Tally's own field as exported. On a Closing Stock ledger whose closing was keyed on Tally's screen the closing field keeps the opening and the keyed change shows only as its debit, so that closing is not the closing stock Tally's statements use. A new check, TB-3, fails if the figure is missing, wrong, or present on a ledger that ties. A trial balance where every ledger ties is unchanged. This is a declared difference from the reference engine (#2133).
