**WHAT IT DECIDES.** `stock_summary` admits only a 31 March `as_of` today. A later change admits any date. It may then return an item inside a stock group with its own row's quantity at that date, which nothing in the read compares (protocol reference, measurements part, §12a.13, the nested-groups bullet of 27 Oct 2026). One book has shown the stock item collection following its `SVTODATE` inside the year, `BRIDGE TIEOUT D` on 26 Oct 2026, but only for one item under the root. The nested book was read only at 31 March 2026, which is also its own period end, so it could not tell a collection that follows `SVTODATE` from one clipped to the company's period. This sheet reads the nested book at two dates around its first Stock Journal (SJ1, 15 Sep 2025), so that the two items SJ1 moved must differ between them. The change admits nested quantities at a date inside the year only if every nested quantity equals Tally's expanded Stock Summary screen at both dates, and the two moved items read the quantity before SJ1 at 31 Aug and after it at 30 Sep. Any one that differs, and nested quantities stay withheld at such dates.
