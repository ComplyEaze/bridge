# Lab run sheet: Profit and Loss and Balance Sheet over a window that spans two financial years (#692). SYNTHETIC COMPANY ONLY. Not for merge.

About 45 minutes. `LAB-COMMON.md` in this folder (v3) applies in full; where this sheet and it differ, the stricter holds. Two Journals are keyed on Tally's screen and removed by a restore at the end; ComplyEaze Bridge writes nothing. Every read is a call of the build's own tools: no XML request is sent by curl (C2 c's and C10's GET /status by `curl.exe` stand).

**WHAT IT DECIDES.** `profit_and_loss` and `balance_sheet` admit a window that crosses 31 March (the checks are only from <= to, licensed mode, and from not before the books' start) and say only that such a window is unmeasured (agent_statements.rs limitations; #692: "windows that span two financial years" not measured). The risk in such a window is the first year's result being dropped or counted twice (`carried` = Profit & Loss A/c closing plus every P&L ledger's closing, reports/statements.rs). This book's 2025-26 net result is exactly 0 (answers 024-025 of 26 Oct), which would hide that, so J0 first gives 2025-26 a non-zero result (+250.00). The sheet then reads the year, April 2026 alone, and 1 April 2025 to 30 April 2026, each beside Tally's own screen. A result that is established and differs from the screen is a product finding; a refusal is recorded as the answer. `stock_summary` takes only `as_of` = a 31 March (others are refused before any request), so it cannot express this window: it is read at 31 March 2026 only, as a control.

**THE COMPANY.** BRIDGE TIEOUT B, GUID `d64eda55-da7c-4106-a171-3e3cb6cdd331` (in the merge gate's synthetic registry), company number 100040 on 26 Oct, books from 1 April 2025, loaded alone; ledgers include Lab Freight (Indirect Expenses), Lab Bank (opening Dr 50000) and Cash (0.00). On 26 Oct its marks read M 218 / V 3. If it is not on the lab PC or `list_companies` shows another GUID: STOP.

**THE BUILD.** Tonight's staged build, master `fd6f7c0d032ec51f9d9de9c80e8bcd33d930ad77` (`bridge_mcp.exe` sha256 `63641231787492201133443cb1a887705c7137f495a9205ffd33c2d0068f7484`), or the build of the 26 Oct reads of this book, master 609776ae (`bridge_mcp.exe` sha256 `4c9bace38a82f77471d9b30d88e20ac92d3472a187b744cfeaf15537edf2df44`); the statement code is the same on both. Hash the exe first and at the end; another hash = STOP. Over stdio, one launch, a new empty `BRIDGE_AGENT_DATA_DIR`, `BRIDGE_TERMS_ACCEPTED=true`; posting, import and redaction unset. MARKS means `ledger_masters` with `company_guid`, `fields` `basic`, first page, no `snapshot_id`: M = `result.snapshot.master_alter_id`, V = `result.snapshot.voucher_alter_id`, `result.snapshot.reused` false. C2 d and C4's MARKS bullet are met by `list_companies` and this read.

**WHO KEYS.** The operator, on Tally's screen, after the run coordinator's yes (given before step 3). A screenshot before each accept; after it, Ctrl+Q back to the Gateway of Tally, then MARKS. Expected screens: F2 for a voucher's date and, inside a report, F2 to change the report's period. Never Alt+F2 (it may change the company's current period). Any other dialog or message = STOP (C7). Never Escape on a voucher screen.

**WHAT TO RECORD FOR EACH STATEMENT CALL** (`profit_and_loss`, then `balance_sheet`, each with `company_guid`, `from`, `to`), saved whole with its sha256: `result.state`, `result.reason`, `result.result` (`gross_result` and `net_result`, or `profit_and_loss.carried` and `.ledger`), `result.closing_stock`, `result.balance_sheet_gate`, `result.tie_out` and the lines; or, for a tool error, `error.code` and its `cause` (for example `profit_and_loss_read_failed` with `statement_trial_balance_row_inconsistent`). "The same results" below means equal except `read_at`, `evidence` and `snapshot`.

**PASS** = every answer whole with its sha256, every window's screen recorded, the comparison of step 8 written for each statement and window, marks unchanged by every read, the book and its current period restored as at step 0. **NOT MEASURED:** an accepted opening stock (step 5 measures the existing refusal), a costing method that changes value, a non-integrated book, a window spanning three years, a window not starting on 1 April, a second year holding stock movements (J0 and J1 move none), `stock_summary` at any date but 31 March, foreign currency.
