## Differences from LAB-COMMON
- C3, client timeout and size bounds: the operator's own requests carry no client timeout, as C3 says. The requests ComplyEaze Bridge sends itself during the tool calls (its company and identity reads, the marks, the ledger and group lists it checks names against and, after the create, one read of the ledgers under `Suspense A/c` filtered by `$Parent`; step 7's call sends none) are NOT the files below, are not seen by the operator, and carry the program's own limits, which the item cannot remove: 20 seconds and 32 MB a request, one at a time; a request that reaches its time limit is not sent again (the company-list read alone is tried up to three times when the connection or the request fails or Tally answers with a server error). So a tool answer that may mean one of them failed or went unanswered = STOP at once: screenshot the whole Tally window, send nothing, call no tool, report; the state reads of C8 are NOT sent (a request the program gave up on may still be running in Tally). Every answer that carries the code `request_deadline_exceeded`, `agent_runtime_read_failed`, `post_queue_read_failed`, `post_masters_unconfirmed`, `post_company_scope_unconfirmed`, `import_dispatch_outcome_unknown`, `company_collection_invalid` or `status_probe_unavailable`, or a value under `verification.read_back` that begins `unavailable`, is treated so, whatever its cause (some of these codes have other causes too). A tool call without an answer in 3 minutes is treated the same. The reads here are small (the largest earlier answer of this company is 56,070 bytes).
- C3, a screenshot after every write and after a NEW SHAPE: the program sends its read-back straight after its own `Create`, inside the one call that sends it, so no screenshot can come between them; the operator's screenshot comes straight after that call's answer and before any request of its own (step 5). The filtered ledger read is a NEW SHAPE for this company: no earlier answer of it exists here, and the `$Parent` filter itself is measured on other books, none with a parent name that holds a slash (`TALLY_PROTOCOL_REFERENCE_MEASUREMENTS_AND_OPEN_QUESTIONS.md` 11e).
- C3, "a write is sent once": the one write is the `Create` that `post_ledger_creates` sends after the Yes of step 5. The operator sends no write file. `post_ledger_creates` is CALLED more than once with the same `batch_id` (steps 4, 5, 7); that is not a second send: the program records its intent before it sends and never sends a batch that has one. Every answer that has a `dispatch` object says `"resent": false` (the same in every answer of this build, so it proves nothing by itself); `"resent": true` = STOP.
- C4, import answer: the counters are read from the tool's answer of step 5 (`counters`), not from a raw answer; the operator never sees the raw import answer. The tool reports seven counters (created, altered, deleted, ignored, errors, cancelled, exceptions); COMBINED, LINEERROR, LASTVCHID and LASTMID do not reach the operator and are not checked in this item.
- C4, MARKS: no MARKS answer of this company carried an ALTVCHID element in the 12 Oct run (23 of 23). Record it as absent or as returned; EXPECT it the same in every MARKS answer of the item (a change = STOP).
- C5: two backups, ML0 before step 2 and ML1 at step 9, each into its OWN new empty local folder (below). C6 restore: none is made in this item.
- C7: the only dialog of ComplyEaze Bridge the item answers is the one titled "ComplyEaze Bridge" followed by a dash and "create one ledger?", with the buttons Yes, No and Cancel. Any other ComplyEaze Bridge window, a second such dialog while one is open, or the dialog appearing when the step says none = screenshot, press no button, STOP. The dialog can open behind another window: bring it forward from the taskbar only, with no click inside Tally. No dialog found within 60 seconds of a call that should show one = press nothing, call nothing, STOP.
- C7, the approval's time limits (the program's own): a `post_ledger_creates` call waits at most 40 seconds for the button and then answers `approval.state` `pending` while the dialog stays open; the dialog closes by itself 120 seconds after it opened, nothing is sent, and the refusal `master_approval_timed_out` in any answer = STOP; a Yes pressed late in its call is not acted on by that call (it answers `approval.state` `approved`) and the NEXT call sends the create; a Yes stays usable for 15 minutes. So after a Yes, any `post_ledger_creates` call with that `batch_id` in the next 15 minutes can send THE WRITE, until an answer has said `attempt_recorded` true. After those 15 minutes the Yes has lapsed: a call then opens a NEW dialog and sends nothing without a new Yes; the item makes no such call without the run coordinator's word. Never call the tool while a dialog is open and unanswered.
- C8, after a STOP: the state reads are the reads the step ends with (MARKS in steps 2, 3, 7, 8 and 9; MARKS and LEDGERS in steps 1 and 4; MARKS, LEDGERS, GROUPS and PARTY in steps 5 and 6), each sent ONCE, at the STOP, only as C8 allows and never after a tool answer of the kind the first line of this list names. They are not sent a second time when the run coordinator answers, unless its word names a read. A tool call is not a state read: after a STOP none is made except on the run coordinator's word ("Stop rules").
- Size bounds (C3), each from an earlier answer of this company: MARKS 8,192 bytes (earlier 3,310); the ledger list 65,536 (earlier 28,054 with 19 ledgers); the group list 131,072 (earlier 56,070); the party read 131,072 (earlier 40,122). ## Stop rules (beyond LAB-COMMON)
Any answer unlike an EXPECT line; a ComplyEaze Bridge dialog the step does not name; a tool answer cut short; a tool the step does not name being called; a company other than THE COMPANY loaded; Educational mode; a second ComplyEaze Bridge with ledger creation allowed. After a STOP the operator calls NO tool: the run coordinator says whether one more `post_ledger_creates` with the same `batch_id` is made. That call only reads when an earlier answer of this batch said `attempt_recorded` true. Without such an answer, a call made within 15 minutes of a Yes can send THE WRITE: it is then the item's one write, it needs the run coordinator's word as a write, and C3's rule for a write holds for it (a MARKS read named in that word and sent just before the call, its answer holding THE COMPANY's row alone, and no key pressed in Tally since).
WHAT THE COMPANY HOLDS IF THE ITEM STOPS (the report says which; nothing else in the book has been keyed, altered or deleted by hand): after steps 0 to 4 the book is as T0 shows it, with backup ML0 from step 1 on; a prepared batch is a line in the data folder and nothing in Tally. In step 5, before the Yes: the same. After the Yes of step 5 and until step 6's reads agree: ONE `Create` of `MLab Live Suspense` may have reached Tally, or may still be sent by a tool call made within 15 minutes of the Yes, and the report gives the time of the Yes and says "sent, outcome as the answer states" with the answer whole; the ledger is not keyed by hand and the batch is not prepared again. After step 6: the book is T0 plus that one ledger. Steps 7 to 10 change nothing in the book.
ROUTE BACK from any STOP: nothing further is keyed, sent or called beyond the state reads of "Differences", C8, and what the run coordinator's word names. On the run coordinator's word to end the item: step 10. No backup is restored unless a restore is ordered (C6); the fallback is ML0.
