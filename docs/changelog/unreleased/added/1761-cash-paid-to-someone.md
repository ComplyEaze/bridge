- A bank cash withdrawal answered `paid_to_someone` is now built instead of refused (#1761). `parse_bank_statement` and `parse_bank_statement_csv` take the answer with two ledgers: `ledger`, the person paid, and `cash_ledger`, the cash-in-hand ledger the cash was paid from. The row becomes two vouchers on its date: a Contra from the bank into `cash_ledger`, then a cash Payment of the same amount to the person paid, whose `bridge_txn_id` is the row's followed by `-cash`. `build_import_xml` checks `cash_ledger` is under Cash-in-Hand and the person paid is not under Suspense A/c. When this file pays one payee more than 10,000.00 in cash in a day, each such Payment's narration is tagged for the CA to check s.40A(3), and the parse counts them in `cash_payments_over_limit`. The tag cites the section as the Income-tax Act, 1961 numbers it. It is a flag only: nothing decides the disallowance, and cash paid to that payee outside the file is not counted. Not yet observed on a live Tally.
