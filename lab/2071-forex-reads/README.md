# Lab run sheet: the three foreign-currency reads on the 0.7.0 build, before and after adding purchases, payments, a part receipt and a rupee control party (#2071). SYNTHETIC COMPANY ONLY. Not for merge.

About 110 minutes: three backups, three ledger masters and seven vouchers keyed on screen in two stages, three rounds of three tool reads and six raw reads. ComplyEaze Bridge writes nothing.

**WHY:** on BRIDGE FOREXGROUP LAB the two sittings of `lab/2071-forex-rerun` (answers on `lab/2071-forex-rerun-answers` and `lab/2071-forex-repeat-answers`) found three refusals. Two of them are now handled in code: the Trial Balance and the compliance ledger read set aside a ledger whose balance is in TallyPrime 7.2's foreign-currency layout (#2141), and the compliance refusal gives a next step (#2124). Neither has been read through the built tools since. The third, `outstandings`, still refuses on the payable report's row for a USD bill whose closing amount Tally sent empty. Only one such row has been seen: a sales bill, settled in full at a different rate. A fix must know which other rows Tally sends that way, and whether a rupee party's bills keep arriving as plain amounts beside them. A book whose only bills belong to set-aside parties leaves the read nothing to cross-check its date against, so the rupee control party is also what lets a fixed read answer with figures.

**WHAT IT DECIDES:**
1. Whether the 0.7.0 build's `trial_balance` and `ledger_masters` (compliance) answer on this book, and which ledgers they set aside (round A, the book as the earlier sittings left it).
2. With a rupee party's part-settled bill added and nothing else (round B1): whether its bill arrives as a plain amount beside the USD party's rows, so that a fixed read has a book where a base party's bill reaches a figure.
3. With the dollar purchases, payments and part receipt added on top (round B2): which bill rows Tally sends with an empty or non-plain closing amount, and in which report, for a purchase bill settled in full at a different rate, a purchase bill settled in full at the same rate, and a sales bill part-settled at a different rate.
4. The same three tool answers in each round, so the code change can be tested on Tally's own bytes. The rupee party is keyed first, in its own stage, so that whatever the supplier rows turn out to be cannot hide its measurement.

**RATES:** every rate keyed in this sheet is a whole number. #2141 recognises a 7.2 composite whose rate is a whole number or empty; a rate with decimals in a ledger's balance is not captured yet and would change a second thing at once. If any amount Tally sends in this sheet carries a rate with decimals, record it whole (it is a finding for #683); the tool reads may then refuse at the ledger classification. That is recorded, not a STOP: finish the round's reads and continue with the next step.
