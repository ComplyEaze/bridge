Only if it is not refused does a box open: then **its text, whole: does it say anything of the two vouchers already in the book, or of X1?** and it is answered **No** by mouse. 4. that nothing was sent: the book holds the same two Contras (not four), the voucher mark did not move, `egress_log` holds no import of X2 (this answers the duplicate question: the post itself checks the book);
5. as an extra read after the No (so it cannot affect 2 to 4): what `verify_import` says of X2, a batch that was never sent whose two rows are in the book as X1's vouchers.
