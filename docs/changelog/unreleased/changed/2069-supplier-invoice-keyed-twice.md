- `gst_by_counter_ledger` no longer totals a supplier invoice keyed twice in Tally (#2069). A Purchase that is `Agst Ref` to a bill another attributed Purchase of the window opened on the same party, with the same counter-ledger amount and tax, is set aside as `bill_already_opened`, and the first one stays counted. Every other attributed Purchase that is `Agst Ref` to a bill stays counted and is listed in `agst_ref_purchase`, so a supplementary invoice is seen, never dropped.
