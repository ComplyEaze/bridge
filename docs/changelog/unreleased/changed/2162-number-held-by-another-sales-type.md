- A posted Sales invoice is now `posted_verified` only when, after its read-back, the invoice number's reads over its financial year, a month at a time as at the build, find one Sales voucher holding its number, of the type it was posted in. A Sales voucher of another type holding the same number now leaves the invoice `posted_divergent` with `invoice_number_in_another_type`, which names that type, and so does a month read that fails, with `invoice_number_read_failed`. Before, the read after the post looked in the posted type alone (#2162).
