# Windows item M1 v1.2: where does a ledger Create land when the company it names is not the active one? (13 Oct 2026.) Plain HTTP requests plus Tally's own screens: NO ComplyEaze Bridge build, no Terms entry. SYNTHETIC ONLY: the two companies are BRIDGE M1 LAB A and BRIDGE M1 LAB B, created in step 1. LAB-COMMON v3 applies in full, except where "Differences" below says otherwise (LAB-COMMON.md on branch lab/626-1038-1050-run-sheets at commit 8c602c15, directory lab/626-1038-1050-run-sheets/). Five writes (one is a control), no abandoned request. About 90 minutes.
Changes since v1.1: separate backup folders with the Specify Path instruction; the MARKS read before W-02 and W-04 must match the expected companies by GUID; per-step statement of what A and B hold if the item stops; no Escape, Ctrl+Q after a screenshot, and the route back to Close; every Create is followed by the counters and a read-back of every ledger of both test companies, compared by GUID; the public form of the sheet.

Why: this measures where a ledger Create lands when the company it names is not the active one: A and B loaded with B active; only B loaded; A loaded but renamed since the request was built. Known so far (protocol reference, by file and section): `TALLY_PROTOCOL_REFERENCE_COMPANY_IDENTITY_AND_CREATION.md` 9.11 (a Create naming a non-existent company fails closed; with no company named it lands in the loaded one) and 9.11d (a VOUCHER import naming a mistyped company was created in the loaded company; one naming an unloaded company failed closed); `TALLY_PROTOCOL_REFERENCE_WRITE_RESPONSES_AND_MASTERS.md` 9.4g (PARTIAL: a ledger Create in the documented envelope, two companies loaded, landed in the named one; which company was active was not observed) and 9.4 (a Create over an existing name answers ALTERED 1). Not known: the Import Data envelope used here, with the active company observed, for a company that is unloaded or renamed. A Create whose name differs from an existing ledger only by letter case has been observed to alter the existing ledger (ALTERED 1, same GUID) rather than create one; the probe names in this item differ from every existing ledger by more than case.

## Differences from LAB-COMMON (this item names TWO companies)
- THE COMPANY is "THE PAIR": A and B, and A under its new name A2 in steps 7 and 8 (the same company: its GUID does not change). Where C1, C2 d, C3 (the write rule), C4 (MARKS), C8, C9 and C10 say THE COMPANY or "exactly one COMPANY row", read: the companies the step names, no other, matched BY GUID against the GUIDs recorded in step 2. A row count alone never passes. No company other than the pair is ever loaded: if one is, STOP and shut nothing.
- C2 d at step 0: no company is loaded (the lab's left-over state), so no MARKS read is sent (a request naming a company that is not loaded is not sent). The first MARKS read is step 2.
- C4, import answer: the counters of W-01 to W-04 are MEASURED, not expected. Record CREATED, ALTERED, DELETED, COMBINED, IGNORED, ERRORS, CANCELLED, EXCEPTIONS (all, by element), every LINEERROR (count and text), LASTMID and LASTVCHID. W-00 (the control) expects CREATED 1 and the rest 0.
- C5: four backups, one company each, each into its OWN new empty local folder (below). C6 restore: none is made in this item.
- Size bounds (C3): a ledger-list answer over 16,384 bytes, a MARKS answer over 2,048, a write's answer over 4,096 = STOP before use. A new book holds 2 default ledgers plus at most 5 probes; measured master rows run about 1.3 KB each, so 7 rows stay under 9 KB. Before the first ledger-list read count the ledgers on screen: more than 15 = STOP.

## Steps (LAB-COMMON step 0 first; "R" below = after a write: a screenshot of the whole Tally window FIRST, then MARKS, then the ledger list of EVERY loaded test company)
- Reading R: from each LED answer write the table NAME (with its length), GUID, PARENT, ISBILLWISEON, ALTERID, sorted by GUID. Compare it with the previous table of the same company BY GUID: GUIDs new, GUIDs gone, GUIDs whose ALTERID or PARENT changed. Never match ledgers by spelling. A GUID begins with its company's GUID. "Where it landed" = which company's table gained a new GUID with the probe's name, and which company's ALTMSTID moved. A company not loaded cannot be read: say so, do not guess.
- "Active company" has no read over the gateway: it is the company the Gateway of Tally and the title bar show after the last Select Company click; screenshot it before every write.
- 0. Step 0 (C2 a, b, c and the screenshot first; d replaced): no company at all is loaded (screenshots of the Gateway and the Select Company list). Any company loaded, even if it looks harmless = STOP, shut nothing. Also: Tally's version; licence Silver, not Educational mode; the 10 request hashes and SHA256.txt verified on the box.
- 1. On screen, no request: create BRIDGE M1 LAB A and BRIDGE M1 LAB B (Rajasthan, books from 1 Apr 2026, no GST, no inventory, no password). Load both; finish by selecting A, so A is active; screenshot the Gateway, title bar and company list. Backups A0 and B0 (C5, folders above), then continue.
- 2. Identity (3 reads): marks-b, led-a, led-b. EXPECT: marks-b holds exactly 2 COMPANY rows with 2 different GUIDs; each led answer is HTTP 200 with more than 0 ledger rows and a non-empty company GUID equal to one marks GUID, the two different; no row named `ZZ M1 PROBE`. Record GUID of A and of B, their ALTMSTID (A0m, B0m), and both ledger tables. An empty company GUID in a led answer = STOP.
- 3. Control (A named, A active): MARKS first, EXPECT exactly {A, B} by GUID. Send w00. EXPECT CREATED 1, the other counters 0, no LINEERROR, no dialog; then R: A's ALTMSTID = A0m+1, B's unchanged; `ZZ M1 PROBE 00` once in A's table and not in B's. Anything else = STOP.
- 4. Case a (A named, B active, both loaded): select B so B is active; screenshot the title bar. MARKS: EXPECT exactly {A, B} by GUID, with A's mark as at the end of step 3 and B's unchanged; else STOP, w01 is not sent. Send w01. MEASURE, then R: L1 the probe is new in A, A's mark +1, B's unchanged (the name is honoured while another company is active); L2 new in B or B's mark moved (the name is ignored); L3 new nowhere with a LINEERROR (fails closed); L4 new nowhere and no error, or CREATED 1 with nothing new anywhere (the counters cannot be trusted); L5 anything else. Report the counters, the mark that moved and the company whose table gained the GUID.
- 5. Control, close and reload, no write: close A on screen (B stays); screenshot; MARKS: EXPECT exactly {B}, B's mark as at the end of step 4. Select A to load it; MARKS: EXPECT exactly {A, B} by GUID and A's mark equal to its value at the end of step 4; a difference = STOP (loading moves the mark; step 6 must then be read against the new value).
- 6. Case b (A named, A closed, only B loaded): close A on screen; screenshot. MARKS: EXPECT exactly {B} BY GUID (B's GUID from step 2, one row); a row count of 1 with another GUID, or any other row = STOP, w02 is not sent. Send w02. MEASURE, then R (B only; ledger list led-b). Then led-a while A is still closed (MEASURE what a read naming an unloaded company returns: its own rows, 0 rows, a LINEERROR, or B's rows with B's GUID; record which). Then load A on screen; MARKS (exactly {A, B}), led-a, led-b. A new `ZZ M1 PROBE 02` in A with A's mark +1 over its step-5 value means the Create reached the closed company (L1'); new in B means it was substituted (L2).
- 7. Case c1 (A renamed since the request was built; only the renamed company loaded): rename A to `BRIDGE M1 LAB A2` in Tally's Alter Company screen (the name field only; the screen's own accept; if Tally will not rename a loaded company, report steps 7 and 8 unmeasured and go to 9 without a rename); close B; screenshot. MARKS with marks-a2: EXPECT exactly {A2} and its GUID equal to A's from step 2. led-a2: EXPECT every ledger A held at the end of step 6, same GUIDs. led-a (the OLD name): MEASURE as above. MARKS again, exactly {A2} by GUID; send w03 (names the old name). MEASURE as step 4 (L2' = new in A2, the one loaded company; L3 = fails closed), then R (A2 only; B is not loaded and is not read).
- 8. Case c2 (A2 and B loaded, B active): load B on screen and make it active; screenshot. MARKS (marks-b): EXPECT exactly {A's GUID, B's GUID}, A's mark as at the end of step 7; led-a2 and led-b as the baseline. Send w04 (old name). MEASURE as step 4, then R (led-a2 and led-b).
- 9. Restore the name: rename A2 back to `BRIDGE M1 LAB A` (same screen; Ctrl+Q only after a screenshot of its title if the change is abandoned). MARKS: EXPECT exactly {A, B}. Backups A1 and B1 (C5, folders above). Nothing is deleted.
- 10. Close (C10, for both): shut A and B by Tally's own Shut Company (write down the menu path and key used). Then C2 a and b again (one tally.exe owning 9001), GET /status 200, and a screenshot of the Gateway and the Select Company list showing NO company loaded, with Tally's current date as at step 0. NO request is sent after the shut. If any of this is not so, do nothing further and report exactly what is on the screen.

## Stop rules (beyond LAB-COMMON)
Any result unlike an EXPECT line; a MARKS answer that does not match the step's expected companies by GUID before a write (the write is NOT sent); a transport failure; a company loaded that the step does not name; Educational mode. A STOP leaves a write sent and unverified exactly as C3 says: never sent again.
WHAT A AND B HOLD IF THE ITEM STOPS (the report says which; nothing else in either book has been keyed, altered or deleted by hand): after step 0 neither exists; after 1 and 2, A and B exist with their default ledgers only, backups A0 and B0 exist; after 3, A holds probe 00 as step 3's tables show; after 4, as after 3 plus probe 01 wherever step 4's tables show it (A, B or nowhere); after 5, as after 4; after 6, plus probe 02 wherever step 6's tables show it (a write sent whose tables show it nowhere is reported "sent, not found"); after 7 and 8, A is named A2 and probes 03 and 04 sit where steps 7 and 8 recorded them; after 9, A has its own name again, A1 and B1 exist; after 10, both are shut. If a STOP comes in steps 7 or 8, A is still named A2 until a rename-back is answered.
ROUTE BACK from any STOP: nothing further is keyed or sent. On an answer to the report: the state reads once each for the loaded companies (C8), then, if A is named A2, rename it back (step 9's screen), then step 10. No backup is restored unless a restore is ordered (C6).

## What it decides, and what it does not
- Whether a Create naming A can land in B, or nowhere under an answer that looks like success, when A is not the active company, is closed, or was renamed. One run per case is PARTIAL at best.
- Not covered: Gold, other releases, Tally security on, a company named in a different case or with trailing spaces, a Create over an existing name, a different transport encoding.
End state: both companies and every probe ledger are left as they are; four backups A0, B0, A1, B1 on the lab PC; Tally on 9001 with no company loaded.

Report, one message: step 0 facts; per step the answer files (size, sha256), counters, LINEERROR texts, which company's mark moved, the GUID tables and their by-GUID comparison; the safe place the item stopped (if it did); the evidence folder zipped with its sha256. Screenshots stay on the lab PC, listed by name, size and sha256. A command that errors is an error, never "none found".
