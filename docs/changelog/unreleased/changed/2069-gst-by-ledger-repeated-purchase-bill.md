- `gst_by_counter_ledger` sets aside a Purchase that may repeat another: one with no bill of its own, entered against the supplier bill another attributed Purchase in the window opened for the same supplier ledger, with the same tax per head. It is listed under `repeats_a_purchase_bill` with its own tax totals and names the other in `repeats`, which stays attributed. On the lab book this is how a supplier invoice keyed twice read, where before it was totalled twice. A revised invoice of equal tax keyed against the original bill would read the same. Not seen: a first copy outside the window or not attributed, a copy with its own bill or none, a copy keyed as a Journal or Debit Note, and a copy with different tax (#2069).
